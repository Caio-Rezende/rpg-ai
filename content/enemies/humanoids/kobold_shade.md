# Kobold Shade

**Type:** Humanoid | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** 1-2
**AC:** 13 | **HP:** 10 | **Speed:** 25 ft. (climbing 25 ft.)
**Size:** Small | **Alignment:** Neutral Evil

## Description

A slender kobold wrapped in a cloak stitched from bat wings and shadow-cloth, making it nearly invisible in dim light. Carries two needle-sharp daggers coated in a paralytic toxin extracted from cave spiders. Where normal kobolds rely on traps, the shade is the trap — an assassin trained to operate behind enemy lines. Equivalent in skill to a halfling rogue.

## Attacks

* **Dagger:** +3 to hit, Melee (5 ft.), 1d6 piercing. Target must succeed on a DC 11 CON save or be Slowed for 1 round due to spider toxin.
* **Throw Dagger:** +3 to hit, Ranged (20/60 ft.), 1d4 piercing. After throwing, the shade can vanish as a reaction if dim light is available (DC 12 WIS Perception to detect its position).

## Behavior

* **Temperament:** Cunning
* **Target Priority:** Isolates and eliminates casters and healers first; avoids engaging melee combatants directly
* **Tactics:** Ambushes from ceilings in caverns (can cling to rock like a gecko); strikes once and vanishes into shadows, reappearing to strike from a new angle
* **Morale:** Flees when below 50% HP or if the party has a creature with darkvision and higher CR. Never fights a solo engagement unless it has guaranteed cover
* **Special Behavior:** Has darkvision (60 ft.). Can hide in dim light even while observed (DC 13 Stealth). Fire-phobic like all kobolds, but will endure fire damage rather than break stealth if an assassination target is in range.
