# Paladin Spell List

Paladins cast spells using **Charisma** as their spellcasting ability. They gain spellcasting at level 2 and have half-caster progression.

## 📖 Spellcasting Progression

| Paladin Level | Cantrips Known | 1st | 2nd | 3rd |
|-------------|---------------|-----|-----|-----|
| 2 | 2 | 2 | — | — |
| 3 | 2 | 3 | — | — |
| 4 | 2 | 3 | — | — |
| 5 | 3 | 3 | 2 | — |
