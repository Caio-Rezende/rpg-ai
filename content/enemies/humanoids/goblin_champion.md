# Goblin Champion

**Type:** Humanoid | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** Solo
**AC:** 14 | **HP:** 16 | **Speed:** 30 ft.
**Size:** Small | **Alignment:** Neutral Evil

## Description

A larger-than-average goblin that wears a crudely forged shield and a notched handaxe, with a painted war mask made from an animal's skull. Its green skin is scarred from duels against rival goblins, and it carries itself with surprising pride. Serves as the brute-enforcer of goblin warbands — equivalent in combat prowess to a human paladin or fighter, though driven by tribal loyalty rather than honor.

## Attacks

* **Handaxe:** +3 to hit, Melee (5 ft.), 1d8 slashing. Can be thrown (20/60 ft.) for 1d6 slashing.
* **Shield Bash:** +2 to hit, Melee (5 ft.), 1d4 bludgeoning. Target must succeed on a DC 11 STR save or be pushed 10 ft. away and dropped prone.

## Behavior

* **Temperament:** Aggressive
* **Target Priority:** Charges the party's frontliner or tank equivalent; shouts taunts to draw aggro away from weaker goblin allies
* **Tactics:** Leads charges and holds the front line; uses Shield Bash to create space for goblin skirmishers to flank
* **Morale:** Fights until no goblin allies remain, then attempts flight. Will stand and fight to the death if its warband's lair entrance is behind it
* **Special Behavior:** Can command nearby goblins (within 30 ft.) to advance or retreat as a bonus action. Immune to being frightened by creatures with CR 1 or lower.
