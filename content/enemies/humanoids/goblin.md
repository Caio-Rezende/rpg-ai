# Goblin

**Type:** Humanoid | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** 3-4
**AC:** 12 | **HP:** 10 | **Speed:** 30 ft.
**Size:** Small | **Alignment:** Neutral Evil

## Description

A stocky, green-skinned humanoid standing three feet tall, with pointed ears, a hunched posture, and yellow eyes that gleam with mischief. Wears patched leather scraps and carries a crude scimitar or rusty spear. Emits a musky odor mixed with campfire smoke. Found in underground warrens, forest clearings, and along poorly guarded trade routes.

## Attacks

* **Scimitar:** +2 to hit, Melee (5 ft.), 1d6 slashing.
* **Javelin:** +2 to hit, Ranged (30/90 ft.), 1d6 piercing. Can only throw one javelin per turn; must sprint 10 ft. to retrieve another.

## Behavior

* **Temperament:** Cunning
* **Target Priority:** Targets the smallest or most isolated party member; avoids heavily armored foes
* **Tactics:** Ambushes from cover; uses hit-and-run tactics — throws javelin and retreats if no immediate ally is nearby to support
* **Morale:** All goblins flee when half their number are defeated. Scream for reinforcements (may draw more enemies to the area) as they retreat
* **Special Behavior:** Naturally suited to dark environments — has darkvision (can see in darkness up to 60 ft.). Can hide in dim light (DC 12 Perception). Drops javelins and tries to flee on horseback if a mount is available.
