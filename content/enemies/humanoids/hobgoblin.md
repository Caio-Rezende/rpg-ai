# Hobgoblin

**Type:** Humanoid | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** 3-4
**AC:** 15 | **HP:** 18 | **Speed:** 30 ft.
**Size:** Medium | **Alignment:** Lawful Evil

## Description

A tall, muscular humanoid standing nearly seven feet tall, with red-tinted skin and a barrel chest. Wears scavenged chainmail over a crimson tunic and carries a well-maintained greatsword. Stands at attention even when idle and barks orders in a guttural tongue. Possesses a military bearing far beyond its intelligence. Found in organized raiding camps, fortified hilltop strongholds, and as mercenaries guarding dangerous places.

## Attacks

* **Greatsword:** +2 to hit, Melee (5 ft.), 1d10 slashing. On a critical hit (natural 20), the target is knocked prone.
* **Discipline:** No attack roll, Ranged (30 ft.). The hobgoblin barks a command. Hobgoblin allies within 30 ft. gain +1 to AC until the start of their next turn.

## Behavior

* **Temperament:** Aggressive
* **Target Priority:** Targets the party's leader or heavily armored combatant first; seeks to break enemy morale by eliminating frontliners
* **Tactics:** Fights in formation — uses Discipline to buff allies, holds center position while smaller goblin/kobold allies flank. Does not chase retreating enemies but methodically advances
* **Morale:** Fights to 25% HP, then attempts a fighting retreat in good order. Will surrender if outnumbered 3-to-1 and has a clear escape route blocked (prefers capture over death — expects honorable treatment)
* **Special Behavior:** Can command goblin and kobold allies as a bonus action (they gain +1 to attack rolls on their next turn). Speaks Common and Goblin. May parley if the party demonstrates martial discipline or superior numbers.
