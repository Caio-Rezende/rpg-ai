# Barkling

**Type:** Treelike | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** 5+
**AC:** 13 | **HP:** 8 | **Speed:** 20 ft.
**Size:** Small | **Alignment:** Neutral

## Description

A small, animated plant creature roughly humanoid in shape but composed of intertwined vines and moss-covered bark. Has glowing green buds where eyes should be and leaves that rustle as it moves. Often found guarding groves, ancient trees, or forest paths. Appears harmless until threatened.

## Attacks

*    **Thorn Whip:** +2 to hit, Melee (10 ft.), 1d4 bludgeoning. On a hit, target cannot move closer than 15 ft. until start of next turn.
*    **Seed Burst:** +2 to hit, Ranged (25 ft.), 1d6 piercing. Target must succeed on a DC 10 DEX save or the seed sprouts, creating difficult terrain around them.

## Behavior

*    **Temperament:** Defensive
*    **Target Priority:** Targets intruders who harm other creatures in its territory; ignores those who pass peacefully
*    **Tactics:** Forms a defensive line to protect forest entrances; uses Seed Burst to slow advancing enemies
*    **Morale:** Stands its ground but will retreat into the deep forest (ending the encounter) if 75% of their group is defeated
*    **Special Behavior:** Treelike type: immune to poison, vulnerable to fire (double damage). Cannot be charmed or frightened. Speaking Sylvan (or showing no aggression) may cause them to stand down and offer safe passage.
