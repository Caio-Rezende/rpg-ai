# Pip

## 👤 Character Profile

## 📜 Traits
*   **Appearance:** Small in stature, even for a halfling, with a presence that feels slightly out of place, as if he's just arrived from a distant land. He wears layered, travel-worn clothing with notably long sleeves that almost entirely cover his hands—a clever way to conceal the quick movements of his sleight-of-hand tricks. His eyes are sharp and observant, always scanning his surroundings.
*   **Personality:** Quiet and enigmatic, he rarely speaks more than necessary, maintaining an air of mystery about his origins. He is highly resourceful and possesses a cautious nature, preferring to observe from the shadows before making his move.

## 🎯 Goals & Motivations
*   **The Family Curse:** Pip is desperately seeking help for a mysterious illness that curses his entire family. He follows the party closely, hoping to earn their trust and confidence so he can eventually ask for their assistance in finding a cure.

## 🛠️ Skills & Abilities
*   **Skills:** Stealth (+5), Sleight of Hand (+5), Perception (+3), Acrobatics (+5), Deception (+3).
*   **Equipment:** Shortsword, Two Daggers, Thieves' Tools, Leather Armor, a small pouch of silver coins, and an enigmatic trinket from his homeland.
