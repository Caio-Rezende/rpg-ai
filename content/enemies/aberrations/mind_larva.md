# Mind Larva

**Type:** Aberration | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** 3-4
**AC:** 10 | **HP:** 8 | **Speed:** 20 ft. (climbing 30 ft.)
**Size:** Tiny | **Alignment:** Unaligned

## Description

A translucent, worm-like creature no longer than a forearm, with pulsing purple veins and clusters of unblinking eyes scattered across its gelatinous body. Leaves a slimy trail that fumes with psychic energy. Found in deep caves, ancient ruins, or places where the veil between planes is thin. Hisses with a sound that echoes inside the mind rather than the ears.

## Attacks

* **Mind Lash:** +2 to hit, Ranged (30 ft.), 1d6 psychic. Target must succeed on a DC 10 WIS save or be Stunned until start of next turn as alien thoughts flood their mind.
* **Burrow:** No attack roll. The larva burrows into a wooden or fleshy surface (AC 5 or lower) as a bonus action, becoming half-hidden (+2 AC while burrowed).

## Behavior

* **Temperament:** Fleeing
* **Target Priority:** Avoids fighters; seeks out casters and perceptive creatures whose mental energy it feeds on
* **Tactics:** Attacks from ceilings and walls in swarms; uses Burrow to escape when things go wrong
* **Morale:** Scatters immediately if half the group is defeated or if hit by thunder damage
* **Special Behavior:** Aberration type: vulnerable to charm and fear. Cannot be frightened by normal sounds (its senses are psychic). Thunderclap spell or sound deals double damage.
