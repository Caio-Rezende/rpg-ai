# Hollow Walker

**Type:** Undead | **CR:** 2 | **Group Size:** 3-4
**AC:** 11 | **HP:** 22 | **Speed:** 30 ft.
**Size:** Medium | **Alignment:** Unaligned

## Description

A translucent, skeletal figure draped in tattered burial shrouds. Its jaw hangs open in a perpetual silent scream and emits a faint blue glow from within its ribcage. Moves with an unnatural lurch, leaving a cold trail. Often found near graveyards, battlefields, or places of tragic death.

## Attacks

*    **Clutch:** +3 to hit, Melee (5 ft.), 2d4 necrotic. On a hit, target takes an extra 1d6 necrotic if they are below half HP.
*    **Wail:** Ranged (40 ft.), no attack roll. All enemies within range must succeed on a DC 12 WIS save or be Frightened until end of next turn. Usable once per combat encounter.

## Behavior

*    **Temperament:** Aggressive
*    **Target Priority:** Targets the weakest (lowest HP) creature first; drawn to injured or dying allies of enemies
*    **Tactics:** Groups fan out to surround prey; uses Wail to break morale before closing in
*    **Morale:** Fights to the death. Does not flee. However, disperses instantly when hit by radiant damage or healing magic.
*    **Special Behavior:** Undead type: immune to necrotic and psychic damage, cannot be charmed. Healing spells (Cure Wounds) deal radiant damage instead. Bright Light spell forces them to retreat 30 ft. and skip next turn.
