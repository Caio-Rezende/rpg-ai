## 📜 Traits
*   **Appearance:** Tall and imposing, with a presence that commands attention, though often accompanied by a somewhat vacant or simple-minded expression. He wears polished steel plate armor that is well-maintained but lacks any complex ornamentation. A heavy shield and a longsword are his primary companions.
*   **Personality:** Disciplined and unwavering in his convictions. He possesses a strong sense of justice and a natural ability to lead others through both words and actions, even if he doesn't always grasp the finer details of a situation.

## 🎯 Goals & Motivations
*   **Legacy of Honor:** Bram is driven by a desire to be remembered as a great hero. He constantly tries to craft the perfect catchphrase to cement his legend and dreams of having a statue erected in his hometown so that his name won't vanish into history like those of forgotten heroes.
