# Rust Golem

**Type:** Construct | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** 1-2
**AC:** 14 | **HP:** 20 | **Speed:** 25 ft.
**Size:** Medium | **Alignment:** Unaligned

## Description

A hulking figure once crafted from iron and bronze, now heavily corroded and pitted with oxidation. Orange rust flakes fall with every jerky movement, and its joints emit a grinding screech. One eye socket holds a cracked ruby that still glows dimly. Found in abandoned forges, ruined towers, and forgotten armories where it continues its original patrol route.

## Attacks

* **Slam:** +2 to hit, Melee (5 ft.), 1d8 bludgeoning + 1d4 bludgeoning from loose metal fragments. Target's metal armor or weapons take 1d6 rust damage (non-magical) — reduce the item's durability by 1 if still functional.
* **Rust Cloud:** No attack roll, Ranged (15 ft.). The golem slams its arms together, releasing a cloud of corrosive dust. All creatures within 15 ft. must succeed on a DC 11 DEX save or take 1d6 acid damage and have their metal armor's AC reduced by 1 until the end of their next turn (non-magical only).

## Behavior

* **Temperament:** Defensive
* **Target Priority:** Attacks intruders in its patrol area; does not pursue targets beyond a 50 ft. radius
* **Tactics:** Holds position or patrols a fixed route; does not flank or retreat — simply swings at whoever is closest
* **Morale:** Fights until destroyed. Does not flee. Collapses into an inert heap of corroded metal when HP reaches 0
* **Special Behavior:** Construct type: immune to poison, disease, charm, fear, and psychic damage. Vulnerable to lightning (double damage). Cannot be healed by normal healing magic.
