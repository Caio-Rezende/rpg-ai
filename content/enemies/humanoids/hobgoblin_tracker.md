# Hobgoblin Tracker

**Type:** Humanoid | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** 1-2
**AC:** 14 | **HP:** 16 | **Speed:** 35 ft.
**Size:** Medium | **Alignment:** Lawful Evil

## Description

A lean hobgoblin scout clad in boiled leather and camouflaged hides, with a longbow carved from ironwood and a quiver of arrowheads tipped with ground glass. Tracks marked with painted symbols on the shoulders identify its hunting pack. Moves through terrain with uncanny silence for its size — equivalent in capability to a human ranger.

## Attacks

* **Longbow:** +3 to hit, Ranged (60/180 ft.), 1d8 piercing.
* **Hunting Knife:** +2 to hit, Melee (5 ft.), 1d6 piercing. Used when an enemy closes to melee range.

## Behavior

* **Temperament:** Cunning
* **Target Priority:** Picks off isolated targets and casters from range; avoids engaging armored melee combatants
* **Tactics:** Opens combat with a longbow shot from maximum range; uses terrain for cover and repositions each turn. If flanked, switches to hunting knife and attempts to disengage
* **Morale:** Retreats when below 50% HP or when out of arrows. Will stand and fight if cornered in difficult terrain (where its mobility advantage is nullified anyway)
* **Special Behavior:** Can track the party through wilderness terrain (DC 13 Survival check to lose). Advantage on Perception checks related to outdoors. If the encounter takes place in forests, hills, or mountains, the tracker starts the encounter hidden and has advantage on its first attack.
