# Brown Viper

**Type:** Animal | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** 1-2
**AC:** 12 | **HP:** 7 | **Speed:** 30 ft. (swimming 30 ft.)
**Size:** Tiny | **Alignment:** Unaligned

## Description

A slender brown snake roughly three feet long, with a pattern of darker diamond shapes along its back that blends perfectly with dry grass and cracked earth. Hisses before striking and can coil tightly enough to squeeze through narrow cracks. Found near riverbanks, rocky outcrops, and warm caverns.

## Attacks

* **Bite:** +2 to hit, Melee (5 ft.), 1d4 piercing. Target must succeed on a DC 10 CON save or take 2d4 poison damage and fall Unconscious for 1 minute. A successful save halves the poison damage; no unconscious effect. Waking creature: any damage or a DC 10 CON check at start of each turn ends unconsciousness.

## Behavior

* **Temperament:** Defensive
* **Target Priority:** Strikes anything that enters its territory; prefers small prey it can constrict alone
* **Tactics:** Ambushes from concealed position in grass or among rocks; strikes and retreats rather than pursuing
* **Morale:** Will not re-engage after a successful strike and retreat; if cornered, fights until incapacitated
* **Special Behavior:** Animal type: takes half necrotic damage, immune to fear. Can hide in natural terrain (DC 12 Perception to spot before it strikes). Immune to being charmed but can be frightened into freezing in place.
