## 🛡️ Armor

Armor Class determines how hard it is to hit a character in combat. Attackers must meet or exceed the target's AC with their attack roll (d20 + attack modifier).

### AC Formula
`AC = Armor Base + MAX(0, DEX Modifier)` — unless the armor prohibits Dexterity bonuses.

### Armor Table

| Armor Type | Category | Base AC | DEX Modifier | Speed Penalty | Weight |
|------------|----------|---------|--------------|---------------|--------|
| **None** | — | 10 | Yes | — | — |
| **Leather Armor** | Light | 11 | Yes | — | 10 lbs. |
| **Studded Leather** | Light | 12 | Yes | — | 13 lbs. |
| **Hide Armor** | Heavy | 14 | No | -5 ft. | 20 lbs. |
| **Scale Mail** | Heavy | 14 | No | -5 ft. | 50 lbs. |
| **Chain Mail** | Heavy | 16 | No | -5 ft. | 55 lbs. |
| **Splint Armor** | Heavy | 17 | No | -5 ft. | 60 lbs. |
| **Plate Armor** | Heavy | 18 | No | -10 ft. | 65 lbs. |

### Armor Categories
| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Light Armor** | Allows full Dexterity modifier to AC. Minimal speed penalty. |
| **Medium Armor** | Allows partial Dexterity modifier (max +2) to AC. Moderate speed penalty. |
| **Heavy Armor** | Does not benefit from Dexterity modifier. May impose speed penalties. Requires armor proficiency. |

> **Note:** Heavy armor (Hide, Chain Mail, Scale Mail, Splint, Plate) does not benefit from Dexterity modifiers. Wearing heavy armor without proficiency imposes disadvantage on ability checks, attack rolls, and saving throws involving Strength and Dexterity, and prevents casting spells.
