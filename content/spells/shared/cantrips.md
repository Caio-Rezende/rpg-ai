# Cantrips (Level 0 Spells)

Cantrips are level 0 spells that can be cast at will, without using a spell slot.

| Spell | School | Casting Time | Range | Duration | Description |
|-------|--------|--------------|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Burning Hands** | Evocation | 1 action | 15 ft. cone | Instantaneous | Flames erupt, dealing 1d6 fire damage (scales with level). |
| **Detect Magic** | Divination | 1 action | 30 ft. radius | Concentration, up to 1 min | Reveal the presence of magic and its school. |
| **Guidance** | Divination | 1 action | Touch | Concentration, up to 1 min | Target adds 1d4 to an ability check. |
| **Light** | Evocation | 1 action | Touch | 1 hour | A shining object emits bright light 20 ft. and dim light 20 ft. more. |
| **Mage Hand** | Transmutation | 1 action | 60 ft. | 1 minute | A spectral hand appears, capable of manipulating objects (up to 10 lbs.). |
| **Mending** | Transmutation | 1 minute | Touch | Instantaneous | Repair a broken object no larger than 1 ft. across. |
| **Message** | Transmutation | 1 action | 120 ft. | 1 minute | Whisper a message to a chosen creature within range. |
| **Prestidigitation** | Transmutation | 1 action | 10 ft. | Up to 1 hour | Create minor sensory effects (cleaning, warming, small flames). |
| **Ray of Frost** | Evocation | 1 action | 60 ft. | Instantaneous | A ray of cold deals 1d6 cold damage and slows the target. |
| **Sacred Flame** | Evocation | 1 action | 60 ft. | Instantaneous | Flaming flames strike a creature, dealing 1d8 radiant damage (no cover). |
| **Spare the Dying** | Abjuration | 1 minute | Touch | Instantaneous | Stabilize a dying creature. |
| **Thaumaturgy** | Transmutation | 1 action | 30 ft. | Up to 1 hour | Exaggerate your voice, glow eyes, or alter appearance slightly. |
| **True Strike** | Divination | 1 action | 60 ft. | Concentration, up to 1 round | Your next attack roll has advantage. |
