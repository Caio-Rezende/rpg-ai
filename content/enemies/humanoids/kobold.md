# Kobold

**Type:** Humanoid | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** 5+
**AC:** 11 | **HP:** 7 | **Speed:** 25 ft. (climbing 25 ft.)
**Size:** Small | **Alignment:** Neutral Evil

## Description

A diminutive, reptilian humanoid barely two feet tall, with scaly brown or copper skin, oversized hands, and large forward-facing eyes adapted to darkness. Carries a crude spear and a slingshot made from woven vine. Despite its cowardly demeanor, exhibits surprising ingenuity in trap design and tunnel construction. Found in cave networks, mountain passes, and underground tunnels.

## Attacks

* **Spear:** +1 to hit, Melee (5 ft.), 1d6 piercing.
* **Sling:** +1 to hit, Ranged (30/90 ft.), 1d4 bludgeoning. Used only as a reaction when a creature enters 30 ft. — cannot attack proactively with sling on its turn.

## Behavior

* **Temperament:** Fleeing
* **Target Priority:** Avoids direct combat; attacks only from prepared positions or when outnumbering the enemy
* **Tactics:** Relies on traps and terrain — triggers pit snares (1d6 bludgeoning, DC 10 DEX to avoid) and collapses tunnels to block retreat. Attacks with sling as enemies walk into kill zones
* **Morale:** Flees immediately if alone or if more than 2 party members are present. Fights only when in a group of 4+ and holding prepared ground
* **Special Behavior:** Has darkvision (60 ft.). Fire-phobic: takes double damage from any fire effect and has disadvantage on saves against fire-based spells. Can be intimidated into surrendering (DC 11 CHA check).
