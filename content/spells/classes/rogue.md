# Rogue Spell List (Arcane Trickster)

Rogues gain spellcasting at level 3 when they choose the **Arcane Trickster** archetype. They cast spells using **Intelligence** as their spellcasting ability.

## 📖 Spellcasting Progression

| Rogue Level | Cantrips Known | 1st | 2nd | 3rd |
|-------------|---------------|-----|-----|-----|
| 1-2 | — | — | — | — |
| 3 | 2 | 2 | — | — |
| 4 | 2 | 3 | — | — |
| 5 | 2 | 3 | — | — |
| 9 | 3 | 4 | 2 | — |

## 🗡️ Arcane Trickster Spells

### Cantrips (Choose 2 at level 3, from Wizard spell list)
- *Blade Ward*, *Chill Touch*, *Friends*, *Ghostly Tickling*, *Mage Hand*, *Mending*, *Message*, *Minor Illusion*, *Mold Earth*, *Prestidigitation*, *True Strike*

> **Note:** At least one cantrip must be from the illusion school.

See [`shared/cantrips.md`](shared/cantrips.md) for full spell details.

### 1st Level (Choose 2 at level 3, from Wizard spell list)
- *Charm Person*, *Color Spray*, *Comprehend Languages*, *Detect Magic*, *Expeditious Retreat*, *False Dawn*, *Find Familiar*, *Illusory Script*, *Magic Missile*, *Protection from Evil and Good*, *Silent Image*, *Sleep*, *Tensile Force*, *Unseen Servant*

> **Note:** At least one spell must be from the illusion or enchantment school.

See [`shared/1st_level.md`](shared/1st_level.md) for full spell details.

### 2nd Level (Choose 1 at level 9, from Wizard spell list)
- *Blur*, *Darkvision*, *Enhance Ability*, *Invisibility*, *Locate Object*, *Mirror Image*, *Misty Step*, *Shatter*, *Suggestion*

See [`shared/2nd_level.md`](shared/2nd_level.md) for full spell details.

---

## 🎭 Signature Spell
At level 9, you choose one 1st-level spell from your spell list. You can cast it once without using a spell slot, and you regain the ability to do so when you finish a short or long rest.
