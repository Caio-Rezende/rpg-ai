# Kobold Charlatan

**Type:** Humanoid | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** 1-2
**AC:** 12 | **HP:** 9 | **Speed:** 25 ft.
**Size:** Small | **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral

## Description

A kobold that has learned to mimic spellcasting through stolen formulae and borrowed magic. Wears a motley of colored scraps and carries a wand that it waves with dramatic flourish — the wand actually works, somehow. Chatters excitedly between attack "spells" as if performing a play. Equivalent in magical capability to a sorcerer or wizard, though its spells are poorly controlled.

## Attacks

* **Flare:** +2 to hit, Ranged (60 ft.), 1d8 fire. A spark erupts from the wand in a bright burst. Target is also Blinded until end of their next turn on a DC 10 DEX save fail.
* **Fumble:** The wand misfires. 1d4 magic missiles (1d4+1 force damage each) spray in a random direction — may hit allies, the charlatan itself, or enemies.

## Behavior

* **Temperament:** Cunning
* **Target Priority:** Targets any creature that approaches within range; has no particular priority beyond "the one closest to me"
* **Tactics:** Keeps maximum distance and waves the wand repeatedly. Tends to monologue between spells (giving the party a chance to close distance). 30% chance each turn that Fumble triggers instead of Flare
* **Morale:** Drops the wand and flees when below 50% HP. The wand can be picked up — it is a +1 wand of Fire, functional for 3 more casts before burning out
* **Special Behavior:** Fire-phobic like all kobolds, making its own fire spells terrifying to itself and nearby kobold allies. If disarmed of the wand, it has no combat capability and immediately surrenders.
