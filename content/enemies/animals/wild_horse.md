# Wild Horse

**Type:** Animal | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** 3-4
**AC:** 10 | **HP:** 15 | **Speed:** 50 ft.
**Size:** Large | **Alignment:** Unaligned

## Description

A muscular, untamed horse standing nearly five feet at the shoulder, with a thick mane and wary eyes. Its coat ranges from dun to roan, marked with faint wild patterns. Moves with restless energy and a low, constant whinny when agitated. Found on open plains, forest edges, and along migratory routes.

## Attacks

* **Hooves:** +2 to hit, Melee (5 ft.), 2d6 bludgeoning. Only effective if the horse is charging (uses its movement to charge at a target). A knocked-down target takes an additional 1d6 bludgeoning.

## Behavior

* **Temperament:** Fleeing
* **Target Priority:** Targets nothing in particular; kicks at whatever is closest when frightened
* **Tactics:** Attempts to bolt past threats rather than fight; will trample small creatures in its panic
* **Morale:** Flees after one successful charge or if it drops below 50% HP. A herd stampedes if more than half attempt to flee
* **Special Behavior:** Animal type: takes half necrotic damage, immune to fear (but cannot be calmed once the herd is spooked). Can be tamed with a DC 13 CHA (Animal Handling) check over 10 minutes. A tamed horse serves as a mount and carries a rider without penalty to speed.
