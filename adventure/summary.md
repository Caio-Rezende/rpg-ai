# Adventure 1 Summary

## 🛡️ The Party
*   **Bram**: A disciplined Human Paladin driven by a strong sense of justice. He is constantly trying to craft the perfect catchphrase, fueled by an ambition to have a statue erected in his hometown so he won't be forgotten like the nameless heroes of the past.
*   **Pip**: An enigmatic Halfling Rogue with sharp eyes. He has been following the group after witnessing their selfless service, hoping to eventually earn enough trust to ask for help with a desperate family curse.
*   **Sariel**: A calm Wood Elf Cleric dedicated to protecting the natural balance. Though she is much older than her companions, her composure often breaks around them, making her appear more like a spirited teenager.

## 🤝 How They Met
The trio was brought together under the auspices of their temple, assigned as traveling servants to perform various chores and aid those in need across distant lands. Pip's decision to join them was sparked by witnessing their dedication to these tasks.

## 📜 Plots
*   **The Unseen Shadow**: Pip follows the group closely, his true motives—a desperate plea for help regarding a family illness—yet to be revealed.
*   **Divine Service**: As temple servants, the party travels from place to place, performing tasks that serve the community and fulfill their divine duties in remote areas.
*   **Nature's Balance vs. Human Order**: Sariel's deep connection to the natural world often creates a subtle tension with Bram's rigid sense of justice and Pip's pragmatic nature.
