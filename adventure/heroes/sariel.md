## 📜 Traits
*   **Appearance:** Tall and powerfully built, with muscles honed by years of training. Her skin has the warm hue of autumn leaves, and her eyes are a striking emerald green. She wears practical leather armor over simple linen tunics, often adorned with small, carved wooden charms. A sturdy mace hangs at her hip alongside a weathered holy symbol made of ancient oak.
*   **Personality:** Calm and observant, possessing a quiet intensity that commands respect. However, she can lose her composure around Bram and Pip, which often makes her appear more like a spirited teenager than an elder race.

## 🎯 Goals & Motivations
*   **Natural Balance:** Sariel is dedicated to protecting the balance of life and fulfilling her divine duties as a temple servant, traveling through remote areas to aid those in need.
