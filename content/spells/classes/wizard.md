## 🔮 Arcane Traditions

Each wizard chooses an arcane tradition at level 2, granting additional features and potentially new spells.

### School of Abjuration
Focus on protection and negation magic. Gains *Arcane Recovery* to regain spell slots.

### School of Evocation
Focus on damage-dealing spells. Gains *Sculpt Spell* to protect allies from area effects.

### School of Divination
Focus on knowledge and foresight. Gains *Portent* to replace dice rolls with pre-rolled results.

### School of Transmutation
Focus on altering properties. Gains *Minor Allegation* for temporary enhancements.
