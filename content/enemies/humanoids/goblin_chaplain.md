# Goblin Chaplain

**Type:** Humanoid | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** 1-2
**AC:** 11 | **HP:** 12 | **Speed:** 30 ft.
**Size:** Small | **Alignment:** Neutral Evil

## Description

A goblin draped in stolen vestments and carrying a maul adorned with teeth and bones. Wears a crudely carved wooden idol around its neck — a dark parody of clerical symbols, dedicated to a forgotten underground deity. Muttering prayers between bouts of violence. Equivalent in capability to an elf cleric, though its "healing" is more crude and its spells lean toward harm.

## Attacks

* **Maul:** +2 to hit, Melee (5 ft.), 1d8 bludgeoning. On a critical hit, the target is knocked prone.
* **Dark Mending:** No attack roll, Ranged (30 ft.). The chaplain slams its idol against the ground. One allied creature within 30 ft. regains 2d6 HP. Alternatively, one enemy within 30 ft. must succeed on a DC 11 WIS save or take 2d6 necrotic damage.

## Behavior

* **Temperament:** Defensive
* **Target Priority:** Keeps back and heals allies; targets healers and casters with its maul if forced into melee
* **Tactics:** Stays behind goblin frontline; alternates between Dark Mending (heal allies) and swinging its maul at anyone who gets close. Prioritizes keeping the Goblin Champion alive
* **Morale:** Fights as long as any goblin ally stands. When all allies fall, drops to its knees and surrenders, clutching its idol
* **Special Behavior:** Can use Dark Mending once per combat encounter. Immune to being charmed. If its idol is destroyed (treat as AC 10, HP 5), it loses access to Dark Mending and fights with panic (disadvantage on all attack rolls).
