# Shadow Imp

**Type:** Demoniac | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** 1-2
**AC:** 13 | **HP:** 12 | **Speed:** 20 ft. (flying 30 ft.)
**Size:** Tiny | **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil

## Description

A grinning humanoid no taller than a child's forearm, with charred reddish skin, leathery bat wings, and a barbed tail that curls like a scorpion's stinger. Its goat-like legs end in hooves that spark when touching stone. Eyes burn with sick amber light and it speaks in a voice like frying fat. Enjoys whispering lies and sowing discord among the unsuspecting. Found near places of corruption, blood pacts, or sites where prayers have gone unanswered.

## Attacks

* **Sting:** +3 to hit, Melee (5 ft.), 1d4 piercing. Target must succeed on a DC 11 CON save or take 1d6 poison damage.
* **Wither:** +2 to hit, Ranged (40 ft.), 1d8 necrotic. On a hit, the target's weapons glow faintly black; reduce the target's attack bonus by 1 until cleansed by a healing spell or short rest.

## Behavior

* **Temperament:** Cunning
* **Target Priority:** Avoids direct combat; targets healers and leaders first to break group cohesion
* **Tactics:** Circles above the fight on bat wings, casting Wither from range; attempts to whisper lies (DC 11 WIS check to resist) between turns, causing allies to attack each other if the check fails
* **Morale:** Flees at 50% HP or when facing a creature with CR higher than its own. Uses a one-round warning teleport (30 ft.) before fully retreating
* **Special Behavior:** Demoniac type: resistant to fire and radiant (half damage), vulnerable to holy damage. Dark aura: creatures within 10 ft. have disadvantage on WIS (perception) checks against deception. Can pass through spaces as small as 1 ft. square.
