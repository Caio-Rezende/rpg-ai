# Weapons Catalog

## ⚔️ Overview

This document contains all available weapons in the game. Each weapon lists its damage dice, damage type, range, and any special properties.

### Weapon Properties
* **Finesse:** Use Dexterity instead of Strength for attack and damage rolls.
* **Ranged:** Attack at a distance (short/long range).
* **Two-Handed:** Requires both hands to wield.
* **Light:** Can be used for two-weapon fighting.
* **Thrown:** Can be thrown at a target (range given in ft.).
* **Reach:** Weapon extends your reach by 5 ft.

## 📖 Attack Rolls

Attack rolls use the character's relevant attribute modifier (Strength for melee, Dexterity for finesse/ranged) plus their proficiency bonus if proficient with the weapon.

* **Formula:** `d20 + Attack Modifier ≥ Target AC`
* **Damage:** On a hit, roll the weapon's damage die + same modifier.
