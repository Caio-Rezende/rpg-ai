# Goblin Skirmisher

**Type:** Humanoid | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** 3-4
**AC:** 12 | **HP:** 11 | **Speed:** 30 ft.
**Size:** Small | **Alignment:** Neutral Evil

## Description

A wiry goblin with a rusted spear and a shield painted with a crude war horn. Dances and chants war beats while advancing, rallying nearby goblins with an energetic rhythm. Where the chaplain prays, the skirmisher performs — drumming on its shield to boost morale and coordinate attacks. Equivalent in group utility to a bard, though its songs are war drums rather than melodies.

## Attacks

* **Spear:** +2 to hit, Melee (5 ft.), 1d6 piercing.
* **War Drum:** No attack roll, Ranged (30 ft.). The skirmisher beats its shield. Goblin allies within 30 ft. gain +1 to attack rolls and can move 10 ft. as a bonus action on their next turn. Has no effect on non-goblins.

## Behavior

* **Temperament:** Aggressive
* **Target Priority:** Stays near the Goblin Champion or highest-CR goblin ally; tries to keep buffed allies in combat
* **Tactics:** Opens with War Drum to buff the charge, then engages in melee. If forced to choose between attacking and drumming, prioritizes drumming when allies are below 50% HP
* **Morale:** Stops drumming and flees when fewer than 2 goblin allies remain. Will keep drumming while retreating to prevent a full rout (goblins retreat in order rather than fleeing panic-stricken)
* **Special Behavior:** War Drum can be used once per combat turn. If killed mid-performance, goblin allies within 30 ft. have disadvantage on their next attack roll (shocked by the sudden silence).
