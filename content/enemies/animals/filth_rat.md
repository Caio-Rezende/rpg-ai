# Filth Rat

**Type:** Animal | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** 5+
**AC:** 10 | **HP:** 5 | **Speed:** 30 ft. (climbing 20 ft.)
**Size:** Tiny | **Alignment:** Unaligned

## Description

A massive, mangy rat twice the size of a normal one, with patchy brown fur, visible ribs, and yellowed teeth. Its small black eyes gleam with instinct and hunger. Found in sewers, cellars, campsites, and anywhere food is left unattended. Moves in skittering groups and makes a chorus of chitters and squeaks.

## Attacks

* **Bite:** +1 to hit, Melee (5 ft.), 1d4 piercing. On a hit, target must succeed on a DC 9 CON save or contract a disease (take 1d4 poison damage at start of each turn for 1 minute; long rest cures).

## Behavior

* **Temperament:** Fleeing
* **Target Priority:** Goes for the nearest target's legs; prefers injured or prone creatures
* **Tactics:** Swarms in from below; bites and retreats to nibble at ankles; distracts larger predators by scurrying away
* **Morale:** Flees as soon as any member takes damage or if more than 2 members are down
* **Special Behavior:** Animal type: takes half necrotic damage, immune to fear. Can be calmed with Calm Animals or lured with food (DC 10 WIS check). Cannot see invisible creatures but can smell and hear them.
