# Star Sprite

**Type:** Celestial | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** 1-2
**AC:** 13 | **HP:** 10 | **Speed:** 0 ft. (flying 40 ft.)
**Size:** Tiny | **Alignment:** Good

## Description

A miniature humanoid no taller than a hand, composed of warm golden light that gives it the appearance of starlight caught in solid form. Delicate wings of radiance flutter at its back, leaving brief sparkles in the air when it moves. Speaks in a voice like distant bells and smells faintly of ozone after a summer storm. Found near sacred sites, under clear night skies, or drawn to creatures in desperate need.

## Attacks

* **Star Tap:** +3 to hit, Melee (5 ft.), 1d6 radiant. On a hit, the target takes radiant damage if evil-aligned; otherwise the sprite offers healing instead of harm.
* **Gleam:** No attack roll, Ranged (30 ft.). The sprite flares brightly. All creatures within 30 ft. must succeed on a DC 11 WIS save or be Blinded until the end of their next turn. Evil creatures take 1d4 radiant damage on a failed save.

## Behavior

* **Temperament:** Neutral
* **Target Priority:** Will not attack good-aligned creatures; targets evil creatures and undead first
* **Tactics:** Hovers at range and uses Gleam to disorient enemies; avoids direct melee unless protecting an ally
* **Morale:** Does not flee but will retreat to a safe distance if outnumbered 3-to-1 or more. Vanishes in a burst of light (teleports away) when dropping below 25% HP
* **Special Behavior:** Celestial type: resistant to necrotic damage. May spare defeated foes, offering them a chance to reform. Automatically allies with the party if they are defending innocents or protecting natural landmarks. Speaking Celestial or demonstrating selfless acts may earn its aid outside combat.
