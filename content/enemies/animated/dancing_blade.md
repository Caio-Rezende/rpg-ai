# Dancing Blade

**Type:** Animated | **CR:** 1 | **Group Size:** 3-4
**AC:** 12 | **HP:** 10 | **Speed:** 25 ft.
**Size:** Tiny | **Alignment:** Unaligned

## Description

A longsword or battleaxe that levitates slightly off the ground, spinning slowly and emitting a soft metallic hum as if tuned to a musical note. Faint runes glow along its blade — remnants of the animation spell that gave it motion. Found in cursed armories, haunted training halls, or places where a bard's mischievous spell went wrong. Clatters against nearby metal objects when idle.

## Attacks

* **Slash:** +2 to hit, Melee (10 ft.), 1d8 slashing. The blade can reach further than its size suggests due to its spinning motion.
* **Spin:** No attack roll. The blade whirls rapidly in place. All creatures within 5 ft. take 1d4 slashing damage (DC 10 DEX save for half).

## Behavior

* **Temperament:** Aggressive
* **Target Priority:** Attacks the creature that drew a weapon first; if no weapon was drawn, targets the largest creature in sight
* **Tactics:** Charges forward and slashes; uses Spin when surrounded to hit multiple targets at once
* **Morale:** Fights until destroyed. When reduced to 0 HP, it collapses into an inert (but undamaged) weapon that can be picked up
* **Special Behavior:** Animated type: immune to charm, fear, and psychic damage. Cannot speak but communicates through tapping and pointing motions. If a bard plays music near it, it may pause its attack for 1 round (DC 11 WIS check by the animating force).
